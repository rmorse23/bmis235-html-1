Tre's Best Current NASCAR Drivers at Each Level

The drivers leading the way at every level of NASCAR

NASCAR has 3 main, televised series, the Cup Series, O'Reilly Series, and the Truck Series. The Cup Series is the best of the three, followed by the O'Reilly Series, then the Truck series.

Most NASCAR drivers work their way up the ladder, starting in the Truck Series, moving up to the O'Reilly Series, and finally earning a ride in the Cup Series. That's why the best drivers at each level are often the future stars of the sport.

The Current Best Drivers in Each Series:

  • Cup Series: Kyle Larson – He won his second title in a row this year, which made him a three-time Cup champion.
  • O'Reilly Series: Justin Allgaier – After more than a decade chasing his first title, he has now won two in three years, including a career-best six-win season in 2026.
  • Truck Series: Layne Riggs – He won more races than anyone else in the 2026 Chase field and finished second in the championship.
